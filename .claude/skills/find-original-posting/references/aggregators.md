# Known aggregators, intermediaries and mirrors

A job from any of the sources below is probably a repost. Find the employer's own posting. Add
new sources here as you meet them, with a note on how to spot them. This list is a starting
point; sites change names and behavior often.

## Aggregators and intermediaries

These repost jobs as their own, and often hide or rename the employer.

| Source | How to spot it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jobgether | "Posted by Jobgether" on a job board; jobgether.com or a Jobgether page on an applicant-tracking host | Hides the company ("partner company"). Often reposts one role under several locations, so the location shown may be the poster's tag rather than the employer's. |
| Talenthop (also written TalentHop) | "TalentHop" as the poster or apply domain | Hides the client company and describes it in a sentence. Check whether it is staffing for an end client. |
| Ladders | ladders.com listings | Often copies an employer posting; may add its own salary estimate. |
| Haystack | "Haystack" as the poster | Hides or relays the employer. |
| RemoteHunter | "RemoteHunter" as the poster or domain | Remote-job reposter. |
| Jooble, Talent.com, Adzuna, Lensa, SimplyHired | Their own domain in the URL, often with a redirect | Search aggregators that scrape other boards; follow the redirect to the source. |
| Dice, ZipRecruiter (some listings) | "Posted by" a recruiter or staffing firm rather than the employer | Often a staffing firm's copy of a client job. |

## Mirrors that are clues, not originals

Remotive, We Work Remotely, Built In, Wellfound, Himalayas, Rise (joinrise.co), Remote OK,
Working Nomads, Jobspresso. These often show the real company name that an aggregator hid, so
use them to name the employer, then go to the employer's own site. A listing on one of them is
never the result by itself.

## Apply routing

Apply links chain. One board's Apply button may open the same job on another board, whose own
button may lead to the employer's page. A one-click or "easy apply" button that keeps you on
the board means that board offers no link to the employer. Follow each hop and record it. The
routing varies by posting, so do not assume where a given board's link goes. When a board runs
the application itself and offers no employer link, search for the employer's posting directly.

## Employer-side hosts

A posting on one of these, under the employer's own name, is usually the original:
greenhouse.io, lever.co, ashbyhq.com, workable.com, breezy.hr, smartrecruiters.com,
myworkdayjobs.com, bamboohr.com, jobvite.com, icims.com, recruitee.com, teamtailor.com,
and the employer's own `careers.` or `jobs.` subdomain.
