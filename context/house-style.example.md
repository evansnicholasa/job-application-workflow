# House style: Alex Example

This is an example. Copy this file to `context/house-style.md` and replace the rules with your
own. The skills apply these rules to résumé bullets, cover letters and fit reviews.

## Prose rules (everything written for Alex)

- Every claim must be defensible out loud in an interview. This outranks every other rule,
  including matching the posting.
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a period or parentheses.
- No epigrams, no sentences that sound like a slogan, no escalating lists of three.
- No character claims ("I am passionate", "I am a natural leader"). Say what was done.
- No "I did X rather than Y" contrasts. State the plain claim.
- Plain words. "Used", not "leveraged". "Helped", not "empowered".

## Résumé conventions

- Keep the headline under the name exactly as the master has it.
- Write "Agile/Scrum", not "Scrum" alone.
- Spell tool names the way their makers do (TypeScript, PostgreSQL, GitHub Actions).
- Years of experience are stated once, on their own ("over 8 years in software quality
  engineering"), never tied to a single tool or activity.

## Cover letter structure

Three short body paragraphs and a closing:

1. The fit: what the posting describes is what Alex has done. Lead with Alex, not with the
   posting.
2. Scale and collaboration: the size of the product, the team, mentoring when the posting asks
   for it.
3. Craft and tooling: lead with whatever stack the posting names.

Closing, two sentences, never cut: "I'd welcome the chance to talk about what I could
contribute to your team. Thank you for your time." Offer a conversation, never promise a
deliverable.

No paragraph about the company's product unless Alex asks for one.

## Length

Up to 280 words. That is a ceiling, not a target. A strong 180-word letter is fine.

## Reference letter

None yet. When Alex approves a letter worth repeating, save it as
`context/reference-cover-letter.md` and name it here as the standard to write toward.

## Rating scale

Score out of 10 and name what is missing.

- 6 to 6.5: accurate and targeted, but reads like the résumé restated.
- 7 to 7.5: everything true and well matched, but could go to any similar posting. The normal
  score without a product paragraph.
- 8 to 8.5: also carries a real, defensible point about this employer.
- Above 9 needs outcome metrics. Alex has none recorded; say so rather than inflating.
